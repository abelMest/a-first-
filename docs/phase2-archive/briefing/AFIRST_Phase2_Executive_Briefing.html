Empirical Rigor

Multi-Signal Gap Detection & A/B Validation

Replacing arbitrary single-score cutoffs with developmental evaluation
The 3-Signal Scoring Formula
Gap Score = (Diagnostic Pre-Test) + (Quiz Errors × 1.5) + min(AI Inquiries × 0.5, 1.5)
  • Signal 1: Diagnostic Placement Factor: +2.0 pts if baseline module score < 70%.
  • Signal 2: In-Course Performance: +1.5 pts per question error in formative checks.
  • Signal 3: AI Tutor Inquiries: +0.5 pts per RAG question inquiry.
  • Curiosity Guardrail: AI inquiries are capped at +1.5 pts so inquisitive learners asking deep questions are never penalized with an artificial gap.
The Herman Tolentino A/B Experiment
100-Learner Monte Carlo Simulation
Variant A (3.0 pts Threshold) — SELECTED
• 93.7% Sensitivity (captures true gaps reliably)
• 89.4% Specificity | 10.6% False Positive Rate
• Catches gaps before clinical or surveillance errors occur.

• Missed over 1 in 4 true gaps! Unacceptable risk in public health.

Presenter Reference

Executive Q&A & Defense Reference

Anticipated questions for Dr. Herman Tolentino & Advisory Reviewers
Q1: Does the AI generate test questions or remedial answers on the fly?
Answer: No, absolutely not. All 27 diagnostic placement questions and all 35 microlearning units are curated, vetted by Africa CDC experts, and locked in the question bank and microlearning catalog. The AI handles diagnostic inference and adaptive routing, eliminating clinical hallucination risk.
Q2: What if a curious learner asks lots of questions? Will they get flagged with a gap?
Answer: No. We engineered a 'Curiosity Guardrail' capping AI inquiry weight at 1.5 points. A gap score cannot cross the 3.0-point threshold without corroboration from actual diagnostic placement errors or formative quiz errors.
Q3: Is this theoretical or tested on actual Moodle?
Answer: It is 100% deployed and validated on our live Moodle 5.0 production VM (10.215.70.14) with full database dumps and system snapshots archived. Complete test evidence is available in the completion report.
